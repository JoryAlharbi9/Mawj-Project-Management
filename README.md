# 🌊 Mawj Project Management System

📌 Overview
Mawj Project Management System is a structured business-focused project designed to analyze and plan a business case through key strategic and financial perspectives. The project applies business analysis methodologies to evaluate feasibility, risks, and market opportunities.

🎯 Project Objective
The main objective of this project is to develop a comprehensive business case by analyzing strategic goals, financial performance, market conditions, and potential risks to support informed decision-making.

🧩 Project Components
- 🧭 Business Case
  Defined the overall business idea, objectives, and value proposition of the project.

- 🎯 Strategic Goals
  Identified key long-term and short-term goals aligned with business vision and direction.

- 💰 Financial Analysis
- Evaluated potential costs, benefits, and ROI to assess project sustainability.

- 📊 Market Research
  Analyzed the target market, customer needs, and competitors to identify opportunities and gaps.

- ⚠️ Risk Analysis
  Identified potential risks and challenges, along with mitigation strategies to reduce impact.

👥 Team Collaboration
This project was completed as a collaborative team effort, with contributions across research, analysis, and strategic planning to develop a complete business case study.
